Module 1: Loose Dirt & Layer Physics Engine
Mechanics Logic
* Layer Mechanics: Loose dirt/sand/dust blocks fall when unsupported, settling into 3x3 layer-spreading configurations (1 layer thick per spread step).
* Stacking Threshold: When 8 layers stack on top of each other after settling, they condense back into a single full solid block.
* Structural Anchors (Bound Soil): Grass_Block, Podzol, Mycelium, Nylium (Crimson/Warped), and Rooted_Dirt act as structural sod anchors. They resist gravity and hold up unsupported raw dirt directly attached to them.
* Raw Dirt Gravity: Standard Dirt, Coarse_Dirt, Mud, and Powdered_Snow collapse into layers if unsupported by an adjacent structural anchor.
Lag Mitigation & World Generation
* Suspended Natural State: During world gen, terrain dirt initializes with a blockstate property: stable: true. Suspended dirt ignores gravity tick updates until disturbed.
* Trigger Conditions: Any player interaction, explosion, liquid flow, or adjacent block state change flips stable: true to stable: false, initiating a cascading neighbor update and potential landslide.
* Player Placement: Dirt placed manually by a player defaults to stable: false instantly.
Module 2: Aspen Tree (Pando) Network Engine
Root Traversal Algorithm
1. Initial Anchor: Standard trees place a $3\times5$ radial disk of Rooted_Dirt beneath the trunk at trunk base height (Y - 1).
2. Pando Underground Creep: Aspen trunks check for expansion via Random Game Tick (RGT) with a 10% success rate:
   * Distance Limit: $10\text{ blocks}$ maximum radial distance from the origin trunk.
   * Target Block: Standard Dirt $1\text{–}2\text{ blocks}$ beneath the surface. Converts target to Rooted_Dirt.
   * Territorial Exclusion: Aspen roots cannot creep into soil already occupied by root blocks of other tree species.
3. Sucker Sprout Checks: Once Rooted_Dirt reaches $\ge 2\text{ blocks}$ away from the origin trunk (up to $8\text{ blocks}$ max for sprouting):
   * Target Surface State: Y + 1 must be a grass-type block (Grass, Podzol, Mycelium), and Y + 2 must be Air.
   * Action: Spawns an Aspen Sapling/Sucker on Y + 2.
4. Decay & Forestry:
   * When an Aspen trunk is chopped, connected Rooted_Dirt enters a decay state, slowly reverting to standard Dirt and triggering gravity updates for supported structures above.
   * Planting a matching Aspen Sapling on decaying Rooted_Dirt instantly consumes the root energy to grow the tree.
Module 3: Termite Colony & Hive-Mind Ecosystem
Hive Capacity & Population Limits
* Units are tracked via a Capacity Unit (CU) system per nest block.
Unit Type
	CU Footprint
	Behavioral Notes
	Standard Nest Block
	$256\text{ CU Total}$
	Houses up to 256 Workers, 16 Soldiers, or mixed ratio.
	Queen's Chamber
	$1024\text{ CU Total}$
	Core hive hub; houses the Queen ($256\text{ CU}$) and royal guard.
	Worker (Particle)
	$1\text{ CU}$
	Single-pixel visual speck; pathfinds to Logs, Wood, Rooted Dirt.
	Soldier (Mob)
	$16\text{ CU}$
	Aggressive defensive unit; climbing mechanics; stacks Formic Acid debuff.
	Queen (State/Entity)
	$256\text{ CU}$
	Lays 1 egg every 3–4 seconds if network capacity permits.
	Hive Alarm & Response Mechanics
* Trigger Conditions: Breaking, hitting, updating, or applying fluid/vibration checks to the Queen’s block triggers a colony-wide alarm.
* Response:
   * All Soldiers within $32\text{ blocks}$ exit Nest Blocks instantly and pathfind directly to the alarm block coordinates.
   * Orphaned State: Killing the Queen halts egg production; surviving Workers retreat permanently into Nest Blocks.
   * New Queen Spawn: If an orphaned nest network remains untreated for $7\text{–}14\text{ in-game days}$, a new Queen evolves in a Nest Block situated $>48\text{ blocks}$ from any active Queen.
* Adaptive Threat Ratio:
   * Peace State: $95\%\text{ Workers} / 5\%\text{ Soldiers}$.
   * High Threat State (Attacked/Collapsing): Dynamically shifts production up to $30\%\text{ Workers} / 70\%\text{ Soldiers}$.
Module 4: Soil Alchemy & Ecological Predators
Chemical & Pungent Interactions
* Miracle Grow: Crafted in a cauldron using Bone Meal + Sulfur Powder + Charcoal Powder + Water. Applying it to roots forces instant sucker sprouting.
* Coconut Oil Proofing: Works like waxing copper. Applied to logs, wood, or dirt to make them permanently immune to termite consumption/traversal.
* Fungal Barriers: Mycelium and Nylium act as natural repellents; termites cannot pathfind through or convert fungal soil.

1. Primary Ores & Refined Metals
Ore Source	Refined Metal	Primary Mod Uses	Golem Type & Role
Plumbum	Lead	Radiation shielding, heavy goggles, gravity-affected armor/blocks.	Lead Golem (Copper): Quarry & non-ore clearing.
Wolfram	Tungsten	Ultra-dense tools/armor (slows player, reduces jump height), heavy machinery.	Tungsten Golem (Copper): Ore miner.
Stannum	Tin	Bronze alloying, basic machinery. Dissolves in Mercury.	Tin Golem (Copper): Fluid & liquid transport.
Cupernikel	Nickel	Structural scaffolding, steel production. Mercury-resistant.	Nickel Golem (Iron): Hazard-immune sentry.
Argentum	Silver	Holy gear, high enchantability (2x Gold durability), magic items.	Silver Golem (Iron): Undead purge sentry.
Cinnabar	Mercury	High-viscosity liquid metal (2-block Nausea aura). Dissolves Al, Sn, Zn. Alloy catalyst.	Quicksilver Golem (Iron): Puddle-tentacle sentry.
Bismuth	Bismuth	Anti-nausea potions, fishing lures, infuses ores to grow crystal buds.	Bismuth Golem (Copper): Crystal cluster harvester.
Bauxite / Native	Aluminium	Scaffolding, infrastructure. Dissolves in Mercury.	Aluminium Golem (Copper): Automated crafter.
Sphalerite / Native	Zinc	Galvanized alloys, logistics machinery. Dissolves in Mercury.	Zinc Golem (Copper): Item sorter & hopper mover.
Neodymium Ore	Neodymium	Magnets, maglev tracks.	Neodymium Mech (Copper): Allay flying suit & magnetic vacuum.
Uraninite	Uranium	Nuclear energy, radioactive components.	Uranium Golem (Copper): Fallout worker & rad-aura sentry.
Orichalcum Ore	Orichalcum	Endgame magical metal. Refined with Quicksilver + Silvered Quartz for Netherite durability + 4x Gold enchantability.	Orichalcum Golem (Iron): Runic ward guardian.
2. Alloys & Special Blends
Bronze: Copper + Tin (Durable early-game utility & Clockwork Harvester Golem).

White Brass: Nickel + Tin + Zinc (Corrosion-resistant machinery & Redstone Buffer Golem).

Electrum: Gold + Silver (High-tier redstone conductors & Lightning Shock-Sentry Golem).

Quicksilver: Mercury + Silver (Fluid magical alloy used for Orichalcum refining and Liquid Sentry Golems).

Quicksteel: Iron + Mercury + Silver + Quartz (High-speed razor-sharp alloy for Quicksteel Dash Golems).

Steel: Iron + Carbon/Coal + Nickel (Upgraded Iron family tank, enchantable via Carved Pumpkins).
